Who We Are

Fostering Ethical, Globally Minded Physicians

The Medical Students Association of Uzima University (MSAUU) serves as a collaborative platform where medical trainees connect, grow, and contribute through education, leadership, research, innovation, and community outreach.

Our Mission

To empower medical students to become competent, ethical, and socially responsible healthcare professionals through education, leadership, and service.

Our Vision

To cultivate a dynamic community of future doctors who are innovative, compassionate, and globally engaged in improving health outcomes.

Institutional Collaboration

MSAUU collaborates closely with national and regional bodies—including the Kenya Medical Association (KMA) and the Medical Students' Association of Kenya (MSAKE)—to provide professional development, mentorship, and clinical opportunities for Uzima students.

Core Architecture

THE FIVE PILLARS OF MSAUU

01 / ACADEMICS

Medical Education

Peer-led tutorials, clinical skills workshops, and revision modules supporting pre-clinical and clinical progression.

02 / SCIENCE

Research & Publications

Fostering research literacy, methodology training, scientific writing, and supporting the Medical Research Club.

03 / OUTREACH

Public Health Advocacy

Direct community health camps, menstrual hygiene pad drives, and preventive healthcare campaigns across Kisumu.

04 / GOVERNANCE

Leadership & Mentorship

Ethical governance training, administrative experience, and career guidance from senior medical practitioners.

05 / INTEGRATION

Professional Collaboration

Connecting students to national medical bodies including KMA, MSAKE, and IFMSA networks.
