Who We Are

Dedicated to Excellence in Medical Training & Community Health

The Medical Students' Association of Uzima University (MSAUU) serves as a collaborative platform where medical trainees connect, grow, and contribute through education, leadership, research, innovation, and community outreach.

Mission

To empower students to become competent, ethical, and socially responsible healthcare professionals.

Vision

To cultivate a dynamic community of innovative, compassionate, and globally engaged medical leaders.

Key Organizational Pillars

  • Student-led academic support & clinical skill building
  • Undergraduate scientific research & journal publications
  • Public health advocacy, pad drives & medical outreach
  • Professional collaboration with bodies like KMA & MSAKE

Our Core Focus Areas

Bridging medical theory and clinical impact across five pillars.

Connecting students with national medical bodies including KMA and MSAKE.

National & Global Framework

MSAKE Standing Committees at MSAUU

Operating under the Medical Students' Association of Kenya (MSAKE) and IFMSA guidelines.

SCOME Medical Education

Medical Education

Focuses on clinical skill building, curriculum enhancement, CMEs, and academic revision.

SCORE Research Exchange

Research Exchange

Drives undergraduate research literacy, methodology workshops, and the Medical Research Club.

SCOPH Public Health

Public Health

Leads preventive medical camps, disease screening, and events like the AMR Minisymposium.

SCORA SRHR & HIV/AIDS

Reproductive Rights

Champions reproductive health, maternal care, HIV prevention, and the Pad Drive Initiative.

SCOPE Professional Exchange

Professional Exchange

Facilitates clinical clerkships and medical mobility across healthcare networks.

SCORP Human Rights & Peace

Human Rights & Peace

Promotes medical ethics, humanitarian action, and equal health access for vulnerable groups.

SCOMH MSAKE Specialized Division

Standing Committee on Mental Health

Dedicated to student mental health awareness, psychological first aid, preventing burnout, and destigmatizing mental care in medical training.

Featured Initiatives & Impact

Real initiatives driven by MSAUU students in Kisumu and beyond.

Community Outreach

Menstrual Health & Pad Drive Initiative

Tackling period poverty by distributing sanitary products, delivering menstrual hygiene education, and supporting young girls to remain in school with dignity.

Academic & Research

Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) Minisymposium

Hosted at Uzima University, bringing together medical trainees and laboratory experts to discuss AMR testing, surveillance, and real-world clinical interventions.

Scientific Literacy

Uzima University Medical Research Club

Launched in collaboration with partners to cultivate undergraduate research culture, host methodology workshops, and support student journal submissions.

Professional Collaboration

Kenya Medical Association (KMA) Partnership

Fostering links with the broader medical fraternity, including KMA support for student lab coats, mentorship, and career transition pathways.
