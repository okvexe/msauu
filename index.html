Who We Are

Bridging Medical Theory and Real-World Practice

The Medical Students Association of Uzima University (MSAUU) serves as a collaborative platform where medical trainees connect, grow, and contribute through education, leadership, research, innovation, and community outreach.

Our Mission

To empower medical students to become competent, ethical, and socially responsible healthcare professionals through education, leadership, and service.

Our Vision

To cultivate a dynamic community of future doctors who are innovative, compassionate, and globally engaged in improving health outcomes.

Core Organizational Focus

  • Medical Education: Peer tutorials, clinical skill enrichment, and academic review sessions.
  • Scientific Research: Promoting undergraduate research literacy and scientific publications.
  • Public Health Advocacy: Menstrual pad drives, community medical camps, and preventive health campaigns.
  • Professional Leadership: Executive training, mentorship, and collaboration with bodies like KMA.

Field Activities & Milestones

Key MSAUU Initiatives

Real stories of student leadership, academic engagement, and service.

Community Outreach

Menstrual Health & Pad Drive Initiative

Fighting period poverty around Kisumu by collecting and distributing sanitary products, delivering menstrual hygiene education, and advocating for schoolgirl dignity and retention.

Academic & Public Health

AMR Minisymposium

Hosted at Uzima University, bringing together medical trainees and laboratory specialists to address Antimicrobial Resistance, testing surveillance, and clinical stewardship.

Research Literacy

Uzima Medical Research Club

Formally launched to cultivate undergraduate research capabilities, methodology workshops, journal clubs, and student scientific publishing.

Professional Integration

Kenya Medical Association (KMA) Partnership

Strengthening links with the senior medical fraternity, featuring KMA support for student lab coats, professional mentorship, and career transition programs.

National Leadership

MSAKE General Assembly Hosting

Selected as the host chapter for the MSAKE General Assembly (February 2026), convening medical student delegates from across Kenya for national governance and policy formulation.

Student Life & Celebrations

Graduation Celebrations & Student Fun Days

Honoring milestones like the MBChB Class of 2025 graduation alongside organized student wellbeing events, sports days, and peer bonding activities.
