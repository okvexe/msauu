Our Mission

To empower medical students to become competent, ethical, and socially responsible healthcare professionals through education, leadership, and service.

Our Vision

To cultivate a dynamic community of future doctors who are innovative, compassionate, and globally engaged in improving health outcomes.

Institutional Collaboration

MSAUU collaborates closely with national and regional bodies, including the Kenya Medical Association (KMA) and the Medical Students’ Association of Kenya (MSAKE), to provide professional development, mentorship, and clinical opportunities for Uzima students.

National & Global Framework

MSAKE Standing Committees

Promotes maternal health, reproductive rights, HIV/AIDS prevention, gender equality advocacy, and drives MSAUU’s signature Menstrual Pad Drive Initiative across local schools.

Uzima LORA Office

Champions medical ethics, health rights for vulnerable populations, refugee aid response, and peacebuilding advocacy in healthcare training.

Uzima LORP Office

Dedicated to medical student psychological well-being, peer support networks, burnout prevention, and fighting mental health stigma in medical training.

Uzima LOMH Office

Leads preventive medical camps, lifestyle disease screening, public health advocacy, and events like the landmark AMR Minisymposium.

Uzima LOPH Office

Drives undergraduate scientific literacy, methodology training workshops, journal reviews, and supports the Uzima Medical Research Club.

Uzima LORE Office

Coordinates clinical clerkships, hospital attachments, and international/national medical student mobility programs.

Uzima LORO Office

Advocates for medical curriculum reform, peer-led teaching, clinical skills workshops, revision modules, and educational advocacy.
